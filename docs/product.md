# Product

GenCut is an AI marketing department for real-estate agents and brokers. The user says
what they want ("more listings in Miami Beach") and GenCut does the work: finds
opportunities, researches them, builds a complete campaign with Film Crew media, and
queues the outreach for one-tap approval.

## MVP loop

1. **Onboard** — market, specialty, brokerage, brand.
2. **Discover** — scored opportunities from public property records and the agent's own
   lists, each with a plain-language reason and its sources.
3. **Research and strategy** — why this opportunity matters and how to approach it.
4. **Campaign** — brief, 30–45 s branded video, captions, social posts, email, letter,
   follow-up sequence, delivered as one package.
5. **Approve** — one tap on desktop, mobile or WhatsApp.
6. **Execute** — queued actions pass the compliance gate; mail and calls become tasks.
7. **Track** — replies, appointments, listings; weekly recommendations.

## Users

Agents, brokers, teams and boutique brokerages in Miami-Dade first, then Broward and
Palm Beach.

## Interfaces

- **PWA** — mobile is the command center (alerts, approvals, quick actions); desktop is
  the full workspace (Overview, Opportunities, Campaigns, Pipeline, Results, Settings).
  Film Crew progress lives inside each campaign.
- **WhatsApp** — a task-specific assistant for linked users: what's new, best opportunity,
  create a campaign, approve, follow-ups. Rich views deep-link into the PWA.

## Out of scope for MVP

SMS outreach, WhatsApp messages to prospects, skip-tracing, MLS data, generative video of
specific real properties, CRM integrations, billing, multi-office brokerages.
